Before you lock it in

  • The Palermo flight day is the pivot point. The direct Edinburgh → Palermo flight runs only ~2 days a week (morning departure). If June 1 isn't one of them, shift your Edinburgh checkout by a day or take a connecting flight — confirm this before booking anything else.
  • You're travelling over UK half-term. The last week of May is a school holiday (bank holiday Mon May 31), so UK lodging and castle tickets run busier and pricier — book early. Starting in early May instead would dodge this and save a few hundred dollars.
  • Entry paperwork. Everyone needs a UK ETA (~£20 each, kids included), and Italy will likely require ETIAS by 2027 (~€20, adults only). Both are quick online forms — apply about a month out.
  • Two long Edinburgh day-trips to skip with little ones: Loch Ness and the West Highland line are ~3.5 hrs each way. The Kelpies and Stirling deliver the big-Scotland feeling much closer to base.

Two ways to save

  • Fly Bath → Edinburgh instead of the train. Bristol airport is ~45 min from Bath; budget fares run £40–60/person for a ~1h15 flight, versus a 6.5–7 hr train day. Faster and often cheaper.
  • Book UK trains ~12 weeks out (around early March 2027) — that's when the cheap Advance fares release. Same logic on the Boston flights: 2–3 months ahead is the sweet spot.
